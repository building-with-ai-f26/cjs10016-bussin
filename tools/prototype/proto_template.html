Bussin prototype

An interactive prototype of the NYU shuttle app. The real thing will be a native iOS app built with SwiftUI and Apple Maps.

Try this:

  1. Tap Where to? and pick NYU Tandon.
  2. Compare the trip options and tap one to see its steps.
  3. Close it, then tap a stop on the map.
  4. Tap a bus to see the stops it has left and when it reaches each one.
  5. Tap a route to see every stop and where its buses are.

Real: NYU's routes, colors and stops from Passio GO's public feed. Simulated: the buses, so arrival times are made up but consistent with what you see on the map. The clock runs 4× fast so the buses visibly move.
