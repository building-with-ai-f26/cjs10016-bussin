Bussin privacy

Bussin shows NYU's shuttles live. It has no accounts, and it never asks for your name or email.

Last updated November 13, 2026

Your location

Bussin uses your location to find the stop nearest you and to time your walk to it. Your phone works that out on the phone. When you search for a place or plan a trip, the places involved go to Apple Maps so it can send back results and walking routes. Bussin's maker never receives or stores your location.

You can say no. Bussin still works: it shows every route and bus, and starts from 715 Broadway instead of from you.

What gets counted

Bussin sends simple usage counts to PostHog, an analytics service, so its maker can see which parts of the app people use. That includes:

  • that the app was opened, and whether you allowed location
  • which routes, stops, and buses were viewed
  • when you search, open a place, or star one
  • for a planned trip, which route and which two stops it used

It never includes the address or place you searched for, your coordinates, or anything that says who you are. The counts are tied to a random ID the app makes for itself, not to you.

What stays on your phone

Your favorite places and recent searches are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Where the buses come from

Bus positions come from NYU's public Passio GO feed. Bussin is a student project. It is not an official NYU app, and NYU and Passio do not run or endorse it.
