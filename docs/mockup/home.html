Bussin: home screen

A clickable mockup for approving the look before we build the real app in SwiftUI.

What's real: the NYU routes, their colors and the stops, pulled from Passio GO's public feed on Nov 8. The streets come from OpenStreetMap and are styled to look like Apple Maps.

What's made up: bus positions, arrival times and favorites. The native app will draw on Apple's MapKit instead of this hand-drawn map.

Try it: drag the map, drag the sheet up and down, tap a route to highlight it, and tap the arrow to recenter.
